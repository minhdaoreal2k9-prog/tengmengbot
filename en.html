FREQUENTLY ASKED

What to know before you begin

For a custom setup, contact the team with your device and usage requirements.

Ask on Discord →
How does Panel sign-in work?

You enter through the AutoROK.vn gateway and sign in with Discord. The panel requests basic identity information to find your linked devices.

Can I control it away from my computer?

Yes. Use Discord commands or open the web panel from any device with a browser.

Can tasks be customized?

Yes. Resource thresholds, task groups, and selected behavior can be configured for each account.

How do I pause?

Use the stop command in Discord or the matching panel control, then confirm the returned status.
